About

Every sticker set here is made by three agents, not one giant prompt. The Editorial Thinker researches and fact-checks the column. The Sticker Scriptwriter finds the angle and writes one point per sticker, with a Japanese reaction line. The Master Illustrator draws it and scores it. If the insight is weak, the work goes back to the Thinker — never to prettier artwork.

Minimum publication score is 7/10. The score is shown on each set page — it is an honesty marker, not a boast.

Claims that are reported but unconfirmed are labelled as such, in the stickers and on the page. A picture never states an unconfirmed claim as fact.

The pipeline, the spec and every brief, script and QA note live in the repo: fimtestxyz/sutekka-generator.

New sticker sets appear here automatically once they are pushed — the listing is generated from the repo, not hand-edited.
