About

gobblemon.com turns current-affairs columns into sticker sets — a different way to learn what's happening in the world, and a fun one. One sticker, one point: each set takes a big story and breaks it into eight small pictures you can actually remember.

Every fact on a sticker is checked before it's drawn. If something is reported but not confirmed, the sticker says so — a picture never states an unconfirmed claim as fact.

How to enjoy it

Browse a set, tap any sticker to read the point behind it, and collect the ones you like. Share a sticker with a friend when it explains something better than a paragraph could. Each set is walked through by a small cast of animal friends — a rabbit with a pencil, a frog with a magnifying glass, an owl with a scarf, and more.
